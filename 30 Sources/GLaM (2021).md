# GLaM (2021)

*Full title: "GLaM: Efficient Scaling of Language Models with Mixture-of-Experts"* (~1,300 citations).

## Summary

First MoE at GPT-3-competitor scale in a *decoder-only* LM: **1.2T total parameters** (~7× GPT-3), 64 experts per MoE layer, top-2 routing, ~97B active per token.

## Key results

- Beats GPT-3 on average zero/one-shot across 29 NLP tasks while using **1/3 of GPT-3's training energy** and **half the inference FLOPs**
- Proof that the Switch-style recipe transfers from encoder-decoder T5 to the decoder-only lineage that became LLMs
