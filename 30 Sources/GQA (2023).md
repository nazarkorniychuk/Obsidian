# GQA (2023)

*Full title: "GQA: Training Generalized Multi-Query Transformer Models from Multi-Head Checkpoints"* (~1,700 citations).

## Summary

Two contributions: (1) **grouped-query attention** — the interpolation between MHA and MQA: $g$ KV heads shared among $h$ query heads ($g{=}h$: MHA; $g{=}1$: MQA); (2) an **uptraining recipe** — convert an existing MHA checkpoint by mean-pooling its KV heads into groups, then continue training for just **5% of original pre-training compute**.

## Key results

- **Uptrained GQA ≈ MHA quality at ≈ MQA speed** — closes MQA's quality gap while keeping most of the bandwidth win
- No need to pre-train a separate inference-optimized model

## Impact

The current default: LLaMA-2-70B, LLaMA-3 (8 KV heads), Mistral, Qwen2+, Gemma-2 all ship GQA. Extended by [[Reducing Transformer KV Cache with Cross-Layer Attention (2024)|CLA]] (share across layers too) and superseded at DeepSeek by [[DeepSeek-V2 (2024)|MLA]].
