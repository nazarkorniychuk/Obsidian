# FNet (2021)

*Full title: "FNet: Mixing Tokens with Fourier Transforms"* (~770 citations).

## Summary

The most radical attention ablation: replace self-attention entirely with an **unparameterized 2D Fourier transform** (mix over sequence and hidden dims). No learned token mixing at all.

## Key results

- **92–97% of BERT's GLUE accuracy at 80% faster training** on GPUs (70% on TPUs, seq 512)
- On Long Range Arena: matches the most accurate efficient transformers while being faster than all of them at most lengths

## Why it's in the vault

Calibrates how much of attention's value is just "*some* fixed token mixing" (most of it, on encoder tasks) versus **content-dependent routing** (the last 3–8%, which grows with task difficulty and is why decoders kept real attention). Complements [[Augmenting Self-attention with Persistent Memory (2019)]] (attention can absorb the FFN) — the two ablations bracket what each component actually contributes.
