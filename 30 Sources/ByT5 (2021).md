# ByT5 (2021)

*Full title: "ByT5: Towards a Token-Free Future with Pre-trained Byte-to-Byte Models"* (TACL, ~750 citations).

## Summary

The strongest case *against* the tokenizer: feed the model **raw UTF-8 bytes** (vocab = 256 + specials), no subword segmentation at all. A standard T5 architecture works with minimal modification (heavier encoder, longer spans).

## Key results

- **Competitive with token-level counterparts** at matched parameters despite 4–5× longer sequences
- **Significantly more robust to noise** (typos, casing) and better on spelling/pronunciation-sensitive tasks — exactly where subword models are blind
- Any-language out of the box; removes the entire tokenizer-training pipeline and its technical debt

## The price

Byte sequences are ~4× longer → quadratic attention cost bites; inference slower. This compute tax is why token-free stayed niche while BPE-style vocabularies grew instead (50k → 256k) — but byte/hierarchical-patch models remain the standing alternative whenever tokenizer pathologies (arithmetic, multilingual inequity) matter most.
