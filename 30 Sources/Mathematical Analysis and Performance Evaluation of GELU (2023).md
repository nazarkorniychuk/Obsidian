# Mathematical Analysis and Performance Evaluation of GELU (2023)

## Summary

An independent (non-author) study of GELU, seven years after the original: rigorous treatment of its differentiability, boundedness, stationarity, and smoothness, plus a controlled benchmark against a broad set of alternatives on residual CNNs (CIFAR-10/100, STL-10).

## Key results

- Confirms **GELU's superior performance** over ReLU and other alternatives in the tested settings — independent replication of [[Gaussian Error Linear Units (2016)]]
- Provides the formal property analysis the original paper skipped

## Why it's in the vault

Original-paper claims should never stand alone; this is the independent confirmation for GELU (contrast with Swish, whose replication [[Benchmarking Comparison of Swish vs Other Activation Functions on CIFAR-10 (2019)|partially failed]]).
